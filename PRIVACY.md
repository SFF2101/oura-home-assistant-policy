# Privacy Policy
**Home Assistant — SFF**

**Effective date:** 29 October 2026

## Purpose

This application is a private, non-commercial integration connecting the owner's Oura account to their personal Home Assistant installation.

Its primary purpose is to monitor Oura Ring battery performance, charging status and battery endurance.

## Information Accessed

The application uses Oura's OAuth2 authentication and API.

The underlying Home Assistant integration may request access to account information and health-related data, including sleep, activity, heart rate and other measurements, depending on the permissions granted.

## Data Storage and Processing

Data retrieved from Oura is processed and stored within the owner's private Home Assistant installation.

Data is not intentionally published, sold or distributed to third parties by this application.

Oura's own processing and storage of account information remain subject to Oura's privacy practices.

## Access and Security

Access is restricted to the authorised owner through OAuth2 authentication.

Application credentials are maintained within the owner's Home Assistant environment.

## Revoking Access

The owner may revoke authorisation through their Oura account's connected-application settings and remove the integration from Home Assistant.

Previously recorded Home Assistant data may require separate deletion.

## Contact

Questions concerning this private application may be directed to the contact email registered in the Oura Developer Portal.
